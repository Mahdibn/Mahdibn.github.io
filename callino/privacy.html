Summary

Callino gives you a second phone number for calls on iPhone and Android. To do that, we need an account, your Callino number, and information about the calls you make and receive. We use this data only to provide the service, bill your plan and prevent abuse. We do not show ads, use analytics or sell your data. Calls are not recorded.

3

What we process

Account

Your name and email address, and a user ID. You can sign up with email and password, Sign in with Apple, or Google. If you use Sign in with Apple, you can choose to hide your email; we then only receive a relay address from Apple. We never see your Apple or Google password.

Your Callino number

The phone number you choose, linked to your account.

Calls

  • Call details: the other party's phone number, direction (incoming or outgoing), start time, duration and how the call ended. We show these to you as your call history and use them to count your plan's minutes.
  • Call audio: carried in real time between you and the other party through our telephony provider. It is not recorded or stored by us.
  • Abuse prevention: when you place a call, our server checks that the caller ID is your own Callino number, that the destination is allowed and that you have minutes left. Calls to premium-rate numbers are blocked.

Device

  • Microphone: used only during a call, so the other party can hear you. The app asks for permission first.
  • Push token: a device token from Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) so incoming calls can ring your phone when the app is closed.

Callino does not access your contacts, photos or location.

Legal bases

We process this data to provide the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), to prevent fraud and misuse of phone numbers (Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in a secure service), and where the law requires us to keep records (Art. 6(1)(c)).

4

Service providers

We use these providers to run Callino. They process data on our behalf and only for this purpose:

  • Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC): sign-in, database for your account and call history, and our server functions, which run in Frankfurt, Germany. Firebase privacy.
  • Telnyx (Telnyx LLC, USA): phone numbers, call routing and call audio transport. Telnyx privacy policy.
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, push notifications, and app store payments.

Some of these providers are based in the USA. Transfers are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and otherwise by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Phone calls by nature involve the network of the person you call.

5

Subscriptions

On iPhone, Callino Premium and minute top-ups are purchased and managed entirely by Apple through the App Store. On Android, they are purchased and managed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We receive a signed confirmation of your purchase (product, purchase and renewal dates, and a transaction ID), which our server checks so your plan and minutes are linked to your Callino account.

You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, or on Android in the Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting your Callino account does not cancel your subscription automatically.

6

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no ads and uses no analytics or advertising tools. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

7

How long we keep it

  • Account and call history: until you delete your account.
  • Your number: released 7 days after your subscription ends, or immediately when you delete your account. A released number may later be given to someone else.
  • Server logs (for errors and fraud prevention): kept for up to 30 days.
  • Purchase records: after account deletion we keep only an anonymous record of minute top-ups, so the same purchase can't be credited twice.
  • Our telephony provider may keep call records for longer where telecommunications or tax law requires it.

To delete your account, open Settings → Delete Account in the app. This releases your number and deletes your account, profile and call history right away. If you can't access the app, email us and we will do it for you.

8

Children

Callino is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
