Summary

MediaShot converts, compresses and edits videos, audio and images. You do not need an account. All processing happens on your device: the files you open, the text and music you add, and the results you create are never uploaded to us or anyone else. The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

3

Your files stay on your device

When you open a file, MediaShot makes a temporary working copy inside the app’s private storage, converts it there, and keeps the result until you save or share it. These copies are removed when you choose Clear or Start Over, and any that remain are deleted the next time the app starts. Deleting the app deletes them too.

We never receive your files, their contents or their metadata. Files you save go only to the place you choose in the Files app, and files you share go only to the app or person you pick in the share sheet.

4

Permissions

  • Photos: MediaShot uses the system photo picker, so it only receives the photos or videos you select. It cannot see the rest of your library.
  • Files: the app can only open files you choose in the Files browser or drag into it.
  • Music library: only if you add a song as background music, iOS asks for access. The song is read on your device and mixed into your video there.
  • Tracking: iOS asks whether the app may use your device’s advertising identifier for ads (see below).

You can change any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → MediaShot.

5

Advertising (Google AdMob)

MediaShot shows full-screen ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Ads appear when you choose a file to open and when you save, share or start over with a result. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process:

  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt,
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them.

Google never receives your files or anything you create in the app.

Consent: When you first open the app in the EU/EEA, UK or Switzerland, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform asks whether you agree to personalised ads. iOS then asks whether the app may track you. You can refuse; you may then see non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under About → Ad Privacy Choices (shown when required in your region), and the tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising and access to device identifiers; our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads. Google may transfer data to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

6

No other tracking

Apart from Google AdMob, the app uses no analytics tools and no other third-party services, and we do not sell your data. The open-source FFmpeg library the app uses to read more file formats runs entirely on your device and sends nothing anywhere.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, please direct requests concerning advertising data to Google as well. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
