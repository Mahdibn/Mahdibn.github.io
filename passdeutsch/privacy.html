Summary

PassDeutsch is a study app for the German naturalisation test (“Leben in Deutschland”). You do not need an account. Your learning progress and settings stay on your device. The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. If you subscribe to the ad-free version, no ads are loaded and no data is shared with Google.

3

Data stored only on your device

Your answers, learning progress, exam history, selected federal state and language are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data and it is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

4

Advertising (Google AdMob)

In the free version we show ads using Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process:

  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • the advertising identifier: on iPhone the IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; on Android the Google advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads),
  • information about ads shown and your interactions with them.

Consent: When you first open the app in the EU/EEA, UK or Switzerland, a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform asks whether you agree to personalised ads. On iPhone, iOS then asks whether the app may track you. You can refuse; you may then see non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under About & Sources → Ad privacy settings (shown when required in your region), and on iPhone the tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising and access to device identifiers; our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads. Google may transfer data to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

5

Subscriptions (Apple App Store and Google Play)

On iPhone, the ad-free subscription is purchased and managed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether an active subscription exists. See Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

On Android, the ad-free subscription is purchased and managed entirely by Google Play (Google Ireland Limited). We do not receive your name or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play, on your device, whether an active subscription exists. See the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from the app under Remove ads → Manage subscription.

6

No other tracking

The app uses no analytics tools and no other third-party services, and we do not sell your data.

7

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for the naturalisation test and is not directed at children under 16.

8

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, please direct requests concerning advertising data to Google as well. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
